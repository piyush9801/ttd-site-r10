What we do

Some clients bring us in to handle the entire booking process. Others bring us in to shape a show, build a talent strategy or open up what’s next.

01 / 07

Full-Service Talent Booking

End-to-end talent booking, fully integrated with your team.

We embed as an extension of your team, managing the entire booking process from strategy through execution.

  • Talent sourcing, curation & research
  • Outreach, pitching & negotiation
  • Custom offer letters & positioning
  • Guest pipeline development & management
  • Weekly strategy & alignment meetings
  • Real-time booking trackers & communication
  • Coordination with production, PR, and editorial teams

02 / 07

Consulting

High-level guidance for teams managing booking internally.

We provide ongoing strategic support to shape your guest pipeline, elevate your booking approach, and maximize impact.

  • Guest strategy & show positioning
  • Ongoing advisory & problem-solving
  • Weekly or monthly strategy sessions
  • On-demand guidance as needs evolve

03 / 07

Show Launch & Relaunch

Set your show up for success, or take it to the next level.

We help define, position, and build momentum for new or evolving shows.

  • Show positioning & messaging
  • Guest category development
  • Offer letter & pitch development
  • Industry introduction strategy

04 / 07

Talent Strategy, Development & Host Curation

Shaping shows from the inside out.

We partner at the earliest stages of development to help define the right talent, voice, and direction for a project.

  • Host ideation and curation
  • Talent recommendations aligned with audience & brand
  • Talent attachment & booking (if greenlit)

05 / 07

PR & Host Visibility Strategy

Expanding your presence beyond your own platform.

We help position hosts as talent, increasing visibility, reach, and cultural relevance.

  • Podcast guesting support for hosts
  • Target show identification & recommendations
  • Outreach & booking for host appearances
  • Messaging & narrative development
  • Coordination with external PR teams
  • Visibility planning around launches, campaigns, and key moments

06 / 07

Special Projects & Brand Partnerships

High-impact, short-term engagements.

We support key moments that require focused booking strategy and execution.

  • Brand partnerships & campaign-driven bookings
  • Festival programming (SXSW, etc.)
  • Tentpole episodes
  • Live shows & tours

07 / 07

Global Expansion & Market Entry

Helping creators grow beyond their home market.

We guide international creators and media companies entering the U.S. market, and support U.S. talent expanding globally.

  • U.S. market entry strategy & positioning
  • Cultural & editorial consulting
  • Cross-platform booking strategy
  • Audience development guidance
  • Host visibility within U.S. media ecosystem
