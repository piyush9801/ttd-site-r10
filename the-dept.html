Rima DairiMorris.

Founder & CEO

rima@thetalentdept.com

Rima Morris is the Founder & CEO of The Talent Department and a veteran talent booking executive with over 20 years of experience across television, podcasts, and global media.

She began her career at The Late Show with David Letterman, booked A-list talent at Jimmy Kimmel Live!, and spent nearly a decade at The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, where she became Supervising Producer overseeing all celebrity guest booking.

Rima later built Spotify’s first in-house podcast booking division, leading talent strategy and acquisition for shows including What Now? with Trevor Noah, The Louis Theroux Podcast and many others. She is known for her deep relationships, sharp instincts, and collaborative approach.

02 / 06

RolandCampos.

Co-Founder & SVP

roland@thetalentdept.com

Roland Campos is Co-Founder & SVP of The Talent Department and a veteran talent booker with more than 25 years of experience across radio, podcasting, and entertainment.

His career includes SiriusXM, where he booked for marquee programs including Opie and Anthony, Sway in the Morning, Comedy Gold Minds with Kevin Hart, and Ricky Gervais Is Deadly Serious, before joining Spotify’s podcast booking team. There, he secured guests for leading shows including What Now? with Trevor Noah, Diary of a CEO, and Science Vs.

Roland is known for his deep industry relationships, practical approach, and ability to consistently connect influential voices with culture-shaping platforms.

03 / 06

LisaPhillips.

VP, Sports & Entertainment

lisa@thetalentdept.com

Who knew that memorizing every actor’s name and biography as a child would spark a career spanning more than 20 years in talent relations, partnerships, and booking? Lisa has built a reputation for her deep knowledge of entertainment, sports, and pop culture, turning passion into opportunity. She began her journey in the NBC Page Program, which led to roles working alongside iconic talent on Late Night with Conan O’Brien, Late Night with Jimmy Fallon, and Larry King Now.

Before joining The Talent Department, Lisa spent over a decade at The Players’ Tribune, founded by Derek Jeter, where she led the Athlete Relations and Partnerships team. In that role, she helped world-class athletes share their stories authentically and directly with fans. Lisa’s career reflects a unique ability to connect talent with meaningful storytelling, creating impactful partnerships across media and sports.

04 / 06

AlexaFix.

Senior Talent Executive, Politics & Entertainment

alexa@thetalentdept.com

Alexa joined the firm with a background in consumer data as the former Chief Brand Officer at a leading data company.

Her expertise in consumer brands and data management, paired with her experience as an investor across various industries, gives her a unique business perspective and an expansive network, making her a valuable asset to The Talent Department.

In addition, her involvement in national politics has created relationships across diverse communities and helped develop a deep appreciation for meaningful engagement, service, and shared purpose. Being a connector has always come naturally to her and is a strong asset to the company. Her focus on podcast booking is a natural extension of that path. She values ideas, storytelling, and helping people find the right platforms to share their voice. Alexa’s approach centers on thoughtful, aligned placements that are authentic and create real value for both guests and audiences alike.
